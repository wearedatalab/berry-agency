What is Berry Concept Agency?

Berry Concept Agency is a creative social media agency. It handles each brand’s strategy, content, paid media, creators and TikTok Shop with one team.

What services does Berry offer?

Five: social media, creative, paid media, TikTok Shop and influencer marketing. You can hire just one or combine them in an integrated plan.

What is the work process like?

It has four stages: Discovery, Ideation, Execution and Impact. We start by understanding your business and finish by measuring the results to decide the next step.

Which social networks does Berry work on?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Pinterest and X, plus TikTok Shop, Meta Ads and Google Ads. We choose the channels based on your audience, not the other way around.

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on where your brand is starting from. We work in 90-day cycles: in the first one we get the strategy running, publish, run ads and measure what works.

How do I start working with Berry?

Write to us at marketing@berryconcept.us or call us at (408) 990 5638. In the first conversation we go over your goals and suggest where to start.
